About

Trained in the darkroom. Still watching the light.

Iris Voss makes portraits, documentary work and wedding films for people who don't perform for the camera. The process starts the same way it did fifteen years ago in a college darkroom — watch closely, wait for the unguarded second, print it honestly.

Recent work has moved between coastal weddings in Portugal, long-form documentary projects in the Balkans, and editorial portraits shot on medium format film. Every project, regardless of format, is edited down to the frames that actually happened.
